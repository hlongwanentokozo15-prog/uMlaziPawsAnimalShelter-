Every Animal Deserves a Loving Home

Umlazi Paws Animal Shelter rescues, shelters, and rehomes stray and abandoned cats and dogs across Durban. We rely entirely on donations, volunteers, and adopters like you to keep our doors open.

Meet Our Animals

Who We Are

Founded eight years ago by a small group of local animal lovers, Umlazi Paws has grown from an informal rescue effort into a registered non-profit shelter. Every animal that comes through our doors receives food, shelter, basic veterinary care, and a chance at a new life. Read our full story or meet the animals waiting for homes.
