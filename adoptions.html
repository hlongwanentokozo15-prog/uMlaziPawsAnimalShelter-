Meet Our Animals

Every animal at uMlazi Paws has been rescued from the streets or surrendered by owners who could no longer care for them. Each one has been vaccinated, dewormed, and assessed by our team before being made available for adoption. Below are just some of the animals currently looking for their forever homes.

Buddy

Species: Dog — Mixed Breed

Age: Approximately 2 years

Buddy is a friendly, energetic dog who loves playing fetch and going for walks. He's great with children and would do best in a home with a yard to run around in.

Whiskers

Species: Cat — Domestic Shorthair

Age: Approximately 1 year

Whiskers is a calm, affectionate cat who enjoys quiet spaces and gentle attention. She would suit a peaceful home, ideally without very young children.

Max

Species: Dog — Mixed Breed

Age: Approximately 4 years

Max is a gentle, well-behaved senior dog who's already house-trained and gets along well with other pets. He's looking for a calm home to settle into for good.
