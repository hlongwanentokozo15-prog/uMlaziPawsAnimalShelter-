Our Story

uMlazi Paws Animal Shelter was founded eight years ago by a small group of local animal lovers who began informally rescuing stray cats and dogs in their neighbourhood. What started as a handful of volunteers feeding and sheltering animals in their own backyards has since grown into a registered non-profit organisation, supported by a small paid staff, a dedicated volunteer base, and the generosity of the Durban community.

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome vulnerable animals, while building a caring community around responsible pet ownership.

Our Vision

A Durban where no stray or abandoned animal is left without care, shelter, or the chance of a loving home.

Our Team

Our shelter is run by a small core team of animal care staff, supported by a rotating group of dedicated volunteers who assist with feeding, walking, cleaning, and socialising the animals in our care. Every member of our team shares one goal: giving every animal that comes through our doors the best possible chance at a new life.
